~/home/gabrielp/quick-facts/

Hi ! I'm Gabriel, an apprentice IT Infrastructure & Operations Specialist at Infomaniak Network SA, the friendly ethical cloud provider based in Geneva, Switzerland. A good part of my week goes into learning networking, development, judging big tech, and helping others use and enjoy technology, and I genuinely enjoy doing so.

The rest goes into photography... mostly wildlife and the night sky even if i am bad at both. I'm also strongly advocating for free, ethical and open source software. I also have a full homelab running Proxmox VE, Snowflakes, Immich, AdGuard, Docker and more self-hosted services i forgot i had. I'm also a student at the CFPT. It's a big and confusing school i'm not a fan of, but alright. I am also the founder of The Olynthe Initiative, a small effort to support digital rights and FOSS.

Frequently Unasked Questions (FUQ)

These answers are outdated, and the FR / IT translations are bad. I'll update them when I get the time. Don't rely on them too much, even if they're mostly (if not entirely) correct. Sorry for the inconvenience! If you have any questions, you can reach me in multiple ways, see above.

What does "Olynthe" mean?

"Olynthe" is the French name for Olynthus, an ancient Greek city, commonly understood to mean "wild fig" or "wild fig fruit".
Olynthus was also where independent cities came together as one league, which makes it a fitting metaphor for open source: separate contributors and projects, one community, shared goals. Also: it sounded cool and looked nice as a domain :)

Is your homelab actually that messy?

Well yes. Software-wise and hardware-wise. But it works. That's the whole point. No further questions on this topic, please.

What camera should I buy to shoot like you?

I wouldn't try to shoot like someone else. Still, may I suggest the Nikon Z6III? If you really want that exact same vibe though, look for a Nikon DSLR like mine or a newer model (i.e. D7200 / D750 / D850), though I don't recommend that in 2026, even if it's clearly still usable.

Can I use your photos?

Of course. All of my photos are licensed under GP1, a license I made myself (gp1.olynthe.org), so you can use it too if you want.

Do you ever sleep?

I do. Sleep just hasn't been next door lately.

Why "FUQ"?

It stands for Frequently Unasked Questions. There's no deeper meaning or hidden reference.

What's your greatest weakness?

I overthink things. This makes me slow or look weird sometimes.

Did an AI write this site?

No, if you mean the content. Yes, if you mean most of this site's HTML and CSS.

Why does your tab say "come back :(" when I leave?

I like that you are here. I'm even asking myself "why is this person here". But I have no way to ask.

Why does the footer say © 2048?

I plan ahead. Also, I heard copyright expires.

Can we be friends?

Do I have any valid reasons to say no? The contact section is just above.

Will you be my photographer?

Well i'm not a pro but... Yes, and for free, since I don't photograph for money. Who even does photography for money ? But heads-up: I work or I'm at school all week, so we'd have to talk first. And if getting to you involves trains or such, I might ask you to help with the costs. I think that's a fair thing to ask.

~/home/gabrielp/values/

The values I hold myself to, in no particular order

I know, words are words. Sadly, none of these can be proven right now, right here, but I'm pretty sure (and hope) that you'll realise it if you spend time near me. And if you see me failing on any of these, tell me and I'll correct myself right away!

  • Transparency & honesty

    I'm committed to being honest and sincere about everything, always, even when it could turn against me. From my own point of view, failing at this would be a real shame.

  • Security & anonymity

    Everyone deserves them, always. Not on request, by default. I'm committed to maintaining strict privacy and protecting the digital security of whoever uses my stuff. This also applies to what people tell me, directly or indirectly.

  • No NSFW

    I'm strictly, from the deepest of my heart, against such material and I don't tolerate it. Minor or not, I don't consider it healthy but dangerous. I will never produce, consume or share such content on purpose.

  • Anti-vulgarity

    I'm committed to avoiding bad words and inappropriate, hateful or rude expressions as much as I can. It's a question of morals as much as respect: how I speak reflects who I am and what I stand for.

  • Nature & life

    I'm deeply committed to protecting the environment. I will never pollute on purpose or out of laziness, and I treat every ecosystem and living thing with care. I reduce my footprint whenever possible, through the choices I make.

  • Respect

    Whoever you are, whatever you do, wherever you are, I'll always respect and appreciate you. I'm committed to treating everyone equally, constantly. I also put others before myself when I can.
